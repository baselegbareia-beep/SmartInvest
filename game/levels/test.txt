@name Movement Room
@teaches Every movement case. Nothing here kills.
@hardest 12,5 the three-tile step up: hold jump for 12 frames or more
; Development level, opened with ?level=test. The room for judging how the hero feels.
; Everything can be tried again and again: the room can be walked in both directions.
; What is where, by column (0-based, as everywhere):
;   6-15   steps up of 1, 2 and 3 tiles
;   16-17  the landing of a 5-tile drop
;   18-19  pillar: the way back up to the top (a 2-tile gap, 3 tiles up)
;   23-24  trench 2 tiles wide     28-30  trench 3 tiles wide     35-38  trench 4 tiles wide
;          (each 2 tiles deep, so falling in costs nothing and climbing out is a 2-tile step)
;   43     hole 1 tile wide: a walking hero always falls in
;   47-52  slab over a corridor 2 tiles high; its left end is the ceiling-corner case
;   56-58  platform 3 tiles up     60-62  platform 3 more tiles up, across a 1-tile gap
; The golden cog is on the last platform; the airlock is on the floor at column 54.

@name Debug Room

@hardest 30,10 hop onto the porch while the Cinderpeg is deep in the tunnel, then fire
@requires blaster
@requires thruster
; Development level, opened with ?level=debug. Every letter of the legend, in play order:
;   crystals (G); spikes (S), furnace fire (F) and a coolant pool (W), all crossed by plain jumps;
;   the bolt blaster (B); an Arc Moth (M) bobbing under a grate;
;   a Cinderpeg (C) in a tunnel 1 tile high: it cannot be jumped, only shot from the porch at column 30;
;   a thruster pack (J) at the foot of a shaft 5 tiles deep; the golden cog (T) on top of the block;
;   a second pack and an Arc Moth (H) patrolling between two hanging pillars, for flying practice;
;   the airlock (D) and a last crystal.
; Why both @requires lines hold: the only way to the cog is up the shaft, the shaft is
; reached only through the tunnel, and the tunnel is too low to jump over the Cinderpeg.

@name Moth Shafts
@teaches Arc Moths bob. Wait, then go.
@hardest 36,12 the 3-tile vent swept by a moth (22 / 13), safe for about 98 of every 300 frames
; Level 6 of 10. A moving hazard in the air: wait, then go. Nothing is required:
; every moth can be waited out and the Cinderpeg jumped (the level without its
; blaster is proven as well). 0-based columns and rows; the floor is row 12.
; Five vents cross the floor: pits open to the bottom, ducts overhead whose
; bottom face, 5 tiles above the floor, is the grate. Each vent's moth bobs
; from the grate to the bottom edge (a lane of 8 cells, about 300 frames there
; and back) and is out of every jump's way while it is deep in the pit: about
; 87 frames in a row for the 2-tile vents, 98 for the 3-tile one (measured on
; the live numbers with every press of the jump's open-air window).
; The airlock stands on the way, after the hardest vent; the cog is beyond two
; more vents at the east end, so the last two vents are crossed there and back.
; About 77 tiles.
;   columns 8-10  free: a moth in a slot 1 tile wide in a pillar. Sprue walks
;           under it and watches it bob; it cannot reach him.
;   vents 1 and 2 (columns 14-15 and 22-23): 2-tile gaps (33 / 24), crossed by
;           timing alone. Each moth is in the far (east) column, so the west
;           lip is a safe place to wait and the east lip is not: standing with
;           more than half of Sprue over its edge is fatal when the moth comes
;           by. Walk on after landing; coming back west, wait back from it.
;   column 29  the blaster. A bolt kills a moth that crosses Sprue's row.
;   vent 3 (columns 37-39), the hardest: a 3-tile gap (22 / 13) with the moth
;           in the middle column. It lands on the Cinderpeg's floor (columns
;           40-47): shoot it across the vent first, or time both.
;   column 41  the airlock, on the Cinderpeg's floor, locked: passed on the way
;           out, so the player knows the cog lies further on.
;   vents 4 and 5 (columns 48-49 and 53-54): 2-tile gaps whose moths sweep the
;           far (east) column, with a floor 3 tiles wide between them. Going
;           east: onto that floor 33 / 24, letting go at once or after 4 or 8
;           frames (measured with the protocol of src/core/limits.test.ts),
;           then on from rest 30 / 24. With the moths, a jump pressed at the lip
;           wins after 440 of 600 waits at vent 5 (comfort 424).
;   columns 55-58  the cog, on a block 2 tiles high at the east end (2 up from
;           the face, any press), under a beam (row 8, cols 56-58) that leaves
;           1 tile of headroom over the block. So every jump off it west is a
;           short arc: jump held 1 to 40 frames lands on the floor at cols 55-56
;           from col 57, and back on the block from col 58 (live and comfort).
;           Without the beam a jump held 7 frames or more from col 57 (13 from
;           col 58; comfort 8 and 16) hit the east face of vent 5's duct and
;           dropped into the vent. The way back crosses vents 5 and 4 from
;           their east lips, the unsafe ones: wait back from the edge, then
;           go. Vent 5 west onto the 3-tile floor 25 / 20 (the same letting go
;           after 0, 4 or 8 frames), vent 4 west from rest 33 / 24; with the
;           moths a jump wins after 376 of 600 waits at vent 5 and 340 at vent
;           4 (comfort 404 and 372). Then the Cinderpeg's floor again, and the
;           airlock.
; Differences from the plan (docs/LEVELS.md, section 5):
; - A fifth Arc Moth, in the slot at column 9, so that the first meeting costs
;   nothing (rule 1), and a sixth, in vent 5. Enemies: 6 Arc Moths (M) and 1
;   Cinderpeg, not 4 and 1.
; - The cog is not over vent 4 but on a block at the east end, past a fifth
;   vent, and the airlock is at column 41, not 56: the cog is fetched, and the
;   east lips, the unsafe ones, are met on the way back (the twist). With the
;   cog over vent 4 on the way to the airlock the level was one line east,
;   about 54 tiles, and a short hop crossed the vent under the cog.
; - Vent 4 is 2 tiles wide, not 3, so that the last jump before the airlock is
;   easier than the hardest one (rule 4).
; - The beam over the cog's block: with the moths bobbing, LEFT and JUMP held
;   off the block died in all 300 phases of vent 5's moth (fell or hit), and
;   now lands safely on the floor in all 300.

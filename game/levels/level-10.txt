@name Heart Furnace
@teaches Relight the Heart. Then get home.
@hardest 29,21 the level 4-tile gap on the way out, open below (11 / 2), a sixth of the way along
@requires blaster
@requires thruster
; Level 10 of 10. There and back: every idea of the game, in the order it was taught. Out along the
; floor of the low hall, east; up two shafts to the Heart, a loop under the furnace's roof where the
; cog sits; home along the top catwalk, west, over the flue, and down into the airlock room, right
; over the start. About 170 tiles, the longest route in the game.
; Teach, test, twist: free at the start (the locked airlock stands in its room over Sprue's head,
; 4 tiles up and out of reach, and the drop off the start platform cannot be climbed back, so the
; way home can only be the long way round); costly on the way out (every older hazard in turn, and
; the game's hardest jump among them); combined on the way home, cog in hand (a flight joined by a
; pack in mid-air, a moth over a pit, a Cinderpeg in a tunnel).
; Why the airlock is in a room over the start and not beside it: a death keeps the cog, so the door
; stays open, and puts Sprue back on the start (docs/ARCHITECTURE.md, section 6). An airlock the
; start could reach would let a death after the cog skip the whole way home.
; Why the hardest jump is on the way out: a miss there costs about 30 tiles of route (a spike bed
; and two coolant gaps, 10 to 12 s), not the whole way out. On the way home a death costs everything
; before it, so nothing there is tighter than ordinary.
; What is where, by column and row (0-based), with every window: the table's cell (docs/LEVELS.md,
; section 2), then the reading on this file with the protocol of src/core/limits.test.ts, live /
; comfort, worst of 16 alignments of the run-up.
;   2,17   start, on a platform in a room under the airlock; nothing moves or kills within 7 tiles
;   8,13   the airlock, locked, in its room 4 tiles above the start platform, in view from it
;   9      off the platform's east edge, a drop of 4 tiles into the low hall, marked by crystals; it
;          cannot be climbed back
;   13     a 1-tile spike bed (27 / 24; read 26 / 24), then 3 tiles of floor
;   17-22  a coolant pad 2 tiles wide between two 2-tile gaps, the coolant 1 below. Onto the pad
;          (27 / 24 stopping at once, 23 / 24 after 4 frames, 19 / 21 after 8; read 27 / 24, 24 / 24,
;          20 / 22); off it onto the bank, from a run 26 / 21, from a standstill at its back 28 / 23
;   23-27  the bank: a run-up of 5 tiles
;   28-31  the hardest jump: a level 4-tile gap, open below, 6 clear tiles above (11 / 2; read 11 / 2,
;          12 / 2 from the bank's back). The only one in the game. A miss falls out of the level
;   33,21  the blaster, on the landing bank (cols 32-34)
;   35-46  the corridor under the furnace: lane 1 (cols 36-39) 1 tile high between 1-tile dips (cols
;          35 and 40), lane 2 (cols 41-46) 2 tiles high, a Cinderpeg in each. Shoot the first from the
;          landing bank and the second from lane 1, each in its row; neither can reach the place it
;          is shot from. Into lane 1 and out of each dip: a hop held 3 frames or more. Lane 1 is 1
;          tile high, so no hop passes its Cinderpeg: the corridor comes before the first pack
;          (rule 9) and without the blaster the @requires run says no-route
;   47-48, 51-52  two vents, 2-tile pits open below, each with an Arc Moth (M) bobbing in its far
;          column from the grate to the bottom edge. Vent 1 from a run along lane 2 onto the 2-tile
;          pillar (cols 49-50): 28 / 24 stopping at once, 24 / 24 after 4 frames, 20 / 21 after 8;
;          vent 2 from rest on the pillar, 23 / 21. A running jump over vent 1 wins for 179 frames
;          of every 256 of bobbing (comfort 173); from a standing start on the pillar, vent 2 for
;          159 (comfort 130)
;   55     a 2-tile step up onto the shaft's foot, entered under the shaft's west wall
;   58,19  pack 1 at the foot of shaft 1 (cols 57-59): 6 tiles of climb to the landing, 24 of 80
;          frames left as the feet clear the lip (comfort 16 of 72). The west wall reaches down to
;          the foot, so LEFT may be held with THRUST from the first frame: the wall guides the climb
;          and LEFT carries Sprue onto the landing. THRUST alone drops him back on the foot
;   50,13  pack 2 at the foot of shaft 2, at the landing's west end under a lintel: a shaft 1 tile
;          wide, 6 tiles of climb to the Heart's lower path, 24 of 80 frames left (comfort 16 of
;          72); RIGHT may be held with THRUST from the first frame
;   50-58  the Heart's lower path, under the crawlway: a fire pit 1 tile wide sunk 1 tile (col 54;
;          read 34 / 32, a 1-tile gap); the crawlway overhead only shortens a full jump
;   59,7   the cog, at the foot of a well 1 tile wide at the path's east end: the only way on is up
;          the well, so the cog cannot be left behind. A 3-tile step up from the well onto the block
;          (cols 60-61; read 30 / 30 standing, 38 / 38 from a run, ordinary), then a 1-tile gap
;          rising 2 back west onto the crawlway (35 / 27; read 26 / 22 standing, 24 / 20 running:
;          the frame above caps the jump)
;   48-58  the crawlway, 2 tiles high, west; at its end a drop of 5 tiles (col 48) onto the flue's
;          lip, marked by crystals
;   48,7   pack 3, on the lip where the drop lands
;   31-44  the flue: 14 tiles of fire 1 below the floor, under a roof 3 tiles above the lip (row 4).
;          Pack 4 hangs right under the roof at 37,5, 7 tiles from the lip's edge and 6 from the
;          catwalk: holding LEFT and THRUST carries Sprue along the roof into it. With LEFT held from
;          the lip, the press of THRUST wins for 23 frames (comfort 18): it must come while Sprue is
;          under the lintel (cols 44-45). Too early lifts him against the lintel's face, and an empty
;          tank sets him down on the lip, where pack 3 comes back; too late drops him in the fire.
;          Both legs use most of a tank, so THRUST simply held lands on the catwalk's east end
;   24-30  the catwalk's east end: a run-up of 7 tiles
;   21-23  a level 3-tile gap over coolant 3 tiles down (22 / 13; read 22 / 13), an Arc Moth (M)
;          bobbing in its middle column from the ceiling to the coolant: out of the jump's way while
;          it is deep, as in level 6. A jump from the lip wins after 225 of 340 waits (comfort 244)
;   16-20  the landing, then a 1-tile step up onto the ledge (cols 13-15)
;   3-12   the tunnel, 1 tile high, with a Cinderpeg walking from its mouth (col 12, 2 tiles high)
;          to its west end. It turns at the ledge's face, so it cannot reach the ledge, and the
;          ledge is 1 tile above its row, so no bolt from there can hit it: step down into the mouth
;          when it walks away, and shoot it before it comes back (the tunnel is 10 tiles, inside a
;          bolt's reach). Hop back up onto the ledge to wait again. Stepping down and tapping FIRE
;          wins for 558 of 660 phases of its patrol (comfort 559); the rest are the phases when it is
;          right at the mouth. A different shot from the porches of levels 5 and 9: the place to
;          shoot from is not safe, only the place to wait
;   1-2    off the tunnel's west end, a drop of 6 tiles into the airlock room, marked by crystals;
;          the airlock is 6 tiles east of where Sprue lands
; Crystals: 17 on the route (the start drop, the spike and gap arcs, lane 2, the vents, both shafts,
; the fire pit, the crawlway, the drop onto the lip, the flue, the moth gap, the tunnel, the drop
; into the airlock room); 9 off it (the start room's corner, two high over the catwalk, the
; landing's corner, over the block, high over the coolant pad, in the second vent moth's lane, high
; in the low hall, the airlock room's corner).
; Differences from the plan (docs/LEVELS.md, section 5):
; - The hardest gap is on the way out (cols 28-31), not on the way home: the first build had it on
;   the catwalk at about three quarters of the route, where every miss re-ran the whole way out.
;   The catwalk gap is now an ordinary 3-tile gap with a moth.
; - The airlock is in a room over the start, reached by a drop at the end of the way home.
; - The climb is in two shafts, and the cog sits at the far end of a loop in the Heart: up shaft 2,
;   east along the lower path to the cog, up the well, back west along the crawlway. The plan's one
;   shaft with a moth sweeping its lip was level 9's lift A again, numbers and all.
; - The three moths are all M: two in the vents, one over the catwalk gap. No H moth: its one place
;   in the plan was that shaft.
; - The catwalk's Cinderpeg walks a tunnel 1 tile high and must be shot (see the tunnel above).
; - Four packs, not three: one per shaft, one on the flue's lip, one in mid-air.
; - Lane 1 of the corridor is 1 tile high, not 2: in a lane 2 tiles high a hop held exactly 3
;   frames slipped past its Cinderpeg (47 of 66,000 tries); rule 9 says to lower it then.
; - The route is about 170 tiles, so a clean run is about 60 to 70 s, not 85 s (rule 10); it is
;   the longest level of the game, by route and by the validator's time.
